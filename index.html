The collection

Made slowly, by hand

EDEÈS began with a simple idea: jewelry should be rare because of the craft behind it, not the label on it. Every design starts as a sketch, is cast in small batches and is set, filed and polished by skilled artisans.

We work with hallmarked gold, sterling silver and ethically sourced stones, and we keep each collection small so every piece gets proper attention.
